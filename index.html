Growth triggers — real vs aspirational

Historical conversion rate: poor

Of what management guided in FY23–24, the optical 20% margin, net-debt/EBITDA <2.5x, the 40% attach-rate target and 5G RAN were all missed (Guid). This team announces big and delivers late and light — read every trigger below through that lens. [O]

USD 1.11 bn hyperscaler award OPTIONALITY

Product Award Letter for multi-year optical connectivity supply, FY27–FY29, ~2x current annual revenue. Not a firm PO — "estimated potential value." The single largest swing factor and the biggest concentration risk. [F]

The binding constraint

Utilisation is still capped by raw-material availability — germanium and helium "remain at elevated price points with persistent supply constraints," and management keeps supplier tie-ups confidential (TN). Every margin-ramp trigger above runs through a utilisation gate that management itself says is not yet clear. [O]

What the price already assumes

Reverse-engineered: to justify ₹564 at even a still-premium 30x P/E you need EPS ~₹18.8 → PAT ~₹966 cr — vs TTM ₹243 cr, a ~4x jump. At ~8% net margin that implies revenue ~₹12,000 cr, more than double TTM ₹5,642 cr and well above management's own "exceed peak ₹5,100 cr" framing. [E]

The price already discounts a scenario larger than management guides to. [O]

Variant perception

Consensus believes the Jun'26 20.16% OPM is the new structural run-rate and STL is now a ~20%-margin AI-connectivity compounder worth 35x+ forward.

The evidence is one quarter — against utilisation still raw-material-capped, attach rate at 15% (not 40%), a partly one-off tariff-transition tailwind, and a team that missed this exact margin target twice.

My edge is on price, not story. The story is broadly right and broadly consensus; the market has paid for FY29 in FY26. There's real value in not chasing. [O]

FIIs (+13pp in a year) rotate out; a promoter-pay or Prysmian-verdict headline crystallises the discount. Leading indicator: FII down >3pp in a quarter

Revised verdict under a 100% growth lens

Halving the achieved growth to 100% does not rescue the entry price: two years of it = fair value ≈ CMP, not upside. Real upside needs either 100% sustaining into a third year (implausible on the order book) or the market keeping a 35x+ multiple. Still WATCH WITH HAWK'S EYE at ₹564 — tracking position, don't initiate — 30x on the two-year 100%-CAGR EPS of ₹18.9 is ~₹567 (fair, not cheap); apply a ~30% margin of safety and it turns BUY nearer ₹400, or on the kill-criteria triggers. High growth ≠ cheap when it is already in the price. [O]
